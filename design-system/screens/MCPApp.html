Anatomy
  • Host · placeholder chrome drawn flat in surface, line and muted, no accent, no shadow · [host app] stands for any client that renders MCP Apps
  • Frame · the host's iframe at the width it gives · the app paints bg inside a line-soft edge at radius-lg · compact inset and region, both space-4
  • Composition · eyebrow chips, Insight, two Metrics, the StatusList's top 3, the strip · charts and the action card stay on the full screen
  • Reflow · 400px and wider: Metrics side by side · narrower: one per row · under 640px: list rows drop the size and the tag wraps under the meta
  • Link · g-link at the strip's end with the external arrow · opens the full screen for the same question, with its drawer and tray
  • Not in the frame · the question, built block, follow-up chips, tray, density control and warmth · the host owns the question and the input
