# WarmingUp

What the composition region shows while GitHub computes a repo's statistics for the first time: one calm sentence, then a lane per statistic where a line runs in real time until that count lands and its part of the answer composes in.

- The composer never picks it. The renderer shows WarmingUp in place of the whole composition when a statistic the composition needs comes back 202 (GitHub computes these on request and asks the caller to come back), and keeps it until every one has landed or checking stops. On a first visit it replaces skeletons; nothing under it pretends to load.
- Schema, all set by the system: `repo`; `stats`, the statistics this composition needs in reading order, each `{ id, state, landedAt, cachedAt }` with `id` in `contributors`, `participation`, `commit_activity`, `punch_card`, `code_frequency` and `state` in `counting`, `ready`, `cached`, `missing`; `attempt`; `nextCheckIn`; `elapsed`; `phase`, `warming` or `stopped`. The model chooses nothing here, not even the copy. Density comes from the screen root.
- Schedule: every counting statistic is checked again on one shared schedule, at 0, 4, 10, 18, 28, 40, 54, 70, 90 and 120 seconds. A 200 stops that statistic's checks. After the tenth check the phase is `stopped`.
- Lead: on `bg` above the card, in the slot the Insight will take. The shared warming mark sits on the card edge, centered on the first line; it becomes the unavailable mark when checking stops. The sentence is the `insight` style (16, 19 or 22px), weight 500, `ink`, max 60ch, with the repo in mono at .85em. It starts on the content edge (side padding plus the 1px card border), exactly where the Insight sentence will land.
- Below the sentence, `body` in `muted`, balanced over at most two lines, says what is happening, how long it usually takes and that it happens once per repo; `space-1`, `space-2` or `space-3` below the sentence by density. Below that, `meta` in `muted` gives the attempt and the next check, counted down each second in tabular figures.
- Card: `g-card` with the header "What GitHub is counting" in `card-title` and the ready count in `meta`. One row per statistic: the name in `row-title`, the endpoint and what it counts in `meta` (one line, ellipsis), the lane, then the status. Name column 280, 300 or 320px, status column 96px, column gap `space-4`, `space-5` or `space-6`. No hairlines between rows; the lanes carry the rhythm.
- Lane: time since the first request runs left to right on one axis shared by every row. The elapsed line is 2px `accent` with round ends, broken by a 2px gap at every check, so each segment is one wait and the backoff reads at a glance. A 1px `line` track runs ahead of it. The row's state mark rides the end of its line, filled with `surface` so the line ends under it: warming while counting, good where the count landed, neutral when a cached copy stands in, unavailable when there is none. A settled line turns to `line` and loses the track ahead.
- Grid and axis: `line-soft` gridlines across the lanes, labels in the axis style (10.5px mono, `muted`) in seconds, and "since the first request" under the names. The axis starts at 30s, doubles to 60s at 25s and to 120s at 50s. End labels align to the lane's ends.
- Status: a tag means the statistic has data: `ready` in `good`, `cached` in `muted` on its own 10% tint. Without data the status is plain `meta` in `muted`: counting, not ready.
- Progress: the shared 3px warming bar on the card's bottom edge fills by statistics ready, one share per statistic. It never fills by guessed time, so it sits empty on a first visit. It retracts when checking stops, because nothing is warming any more.
- Density: side padding, row padding, region gap, sentence size and name column follow the density. The lane, marks, axis, status column and type sizes in the card do not.
- Narrow: under 760px of container width (a container query on the region), the lane drops under the name across the full content width, the status stays on the name line, gridlines step back and the axis labels stay.
- First visit: every statistic counting, nothing ready, lead says it is the first time, that it usually takes under a minute and only happens once per repo.
- Retry: the same lanes with more gaps. The lead says repos with a long history can take a minute or two, and that the reader can leave it open. Attempt and next check in meta.
- Partly ready: landed lanes settle with the good mark where they landed; the bar holds their share; the pieces those statistics feed compose in below at the region gap while the rest keeps counting. The lead names what is still counting. WarmingUp keeps its size, so nothing below it jumps.
- Stopped: after 10 checks over 2 minutes. A statistic with a cached copy gets the neutral mark, a `cached` tag (`g-tag quiet`) and the copy's date in its meta, and the pieces built from it carry that date in their own meta. A statistic with no copy gets the unavailable mark, its pieces are left out, and the lead says so. Try again sits in `accent-text` at the end of the lead's meta line. Never `crit`: a slow GitHub blocks nothing.
- Enter: the sentence wipes in (900ms) and the lines under it follow; the card rises 10px over 550ms; rows slide in 70ms apart; each lane's track extends from the left (700ms, `power3.inOut`) and its warming head sets down at 0s.
- Clock: the one continuous motion in the system. Every counting line extends at a constant rate in real time with its head riding the end, linear because it is a clock, about 18px a second on a 30s axis. Nothing loops, nothing spins and nothing bounces: WarmingUp marks no latest or peak point.
- Check: the meta reads "checking now"; every counting line closes its segment, a 2px gap opens and the next segment starts from it; the attempt number counts up and the countdown restarts.
- Update (a statistic lands): the head crossfades from warming to good (200ms), the line settles from `accent` to `line` (300ms), the track ahead retracts into the head (350ms), the status wipes to ready, the header count counts and the bar extends by one share (700ms). The piece that needed the statistic rises in below and its neighbors move with Flip.
- Morph (rescale): when the clock nears the end of the axis, gridlines and labels slide to their new places and every line shortens in place (700ms, `power3.inOut`). Stop: lines settle, heads swap marks, the bar retracts to the left (350ms), and the lead wipes out and the stopped sentence wipes in.
- Exit: when the last statistic lands, the lead wipes out (500ms) as the Insight wipes in behind it; rows slide out to the left top to bottom, lines retract, the card sinks (`power2.in`), and the composed pieces Flip up into the space. Try again that brings everything back runs the same exit.
- Reduced motion: no running lines. At each check every line steps to its new length with a 200ms opacity change; the countdown still changes once a second as plain text; landing, rescale, stop and exit collapse to 200ms opacity changes in the same order.
- Accessibility: the region is `aria-busy` while anything counts. One polite status line speaks on arrival, on each landing and on stop, never every second. Lanes, axis and countdown are hidden from assistive tech; each row reads as name, endpoint and status. Try again is a real button in `accent-text` (5.38:1 on `surface`, 5.16:1 on `bg`) with the 2px `focus` ring at 2px offset (12.52:1 on `bg`); hover takes its underline to full color. Contrast: `ink` 15.55:1 and `muted` 5.67:1 on `bg`; the cached tag 5.14:1 on its tint; the ready tag 5.03:1; the `accent` line 4.84:1 and the good mark 4.09:1 on `surface`. Settled lines are decorative; the mark and the status carry state.
- Copy says what GitHub is doing, how long it usually takes and when the next check is. Never a percentage, a guessed estimate or "almost there".
