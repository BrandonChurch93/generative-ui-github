# ActionCard

One thing the model proposes doing next, stated as its effect, with a primary and a secondary button, an inline second step that restates exactly what will happen, and a mono confirm line that reports the result.

- The composer picks it when the answer points to one small, undoable next step on data already on screen: nudge the reviewers holding a milestone, re-run a failed check, request a review. At most one per screen, and always the last piece.
- Schema, model side: `heading` (verb first, the effect, 48 characters at most), `body` (what happens and what does not, 140 at most), `primary` (the button label, verb first), an optional `secondary` (`{ label, href }` to leave for GitHub, or `{ label, ask }` to re-compose with a follow-up question) and `effect`.
- `effect` is typed: `kind` is one of `comment`, `request-review`, `add-label`, `rerun-checks`, and `targets` are PR or check ids taken from the composition. Nothing that merges, closes or deletes is in the enum, so the card cannot propose an irreversible act.
- Schema, system side: the confirm question, the confirm line, the working count and the result are all written by code from `effect` ("Post 3 comments on #84120, #84077, #84201?"), never by the model, so the restatement cannot drift from what will run. `mode` is `sample` in the portfolio and `live` only when the viewer has signed in with write access. The model never sets mode or state.
- Card: `surface-tint` ground, the only tinted card in the system · `line-soft` border · `radius-lg` · `card` shadow · padding is the density's card top and bottom by side padding, so the heading sits level with any card header beside it.
- Heading: the `action` style, 15px 600 in ink. Copy: the `body` style, 14px 400 in muted (5.48:1 on surface-tint), max 60ch, `space-1` below the heading (`space-2` spacious).
- Buttons: 36, 40 or 44px tall by density, `radius-md`, label 13px 500 (14px spacious), `space-2` apart. Primary is the `accent` to `accent-2` gradient at 135deg with an `accent-ink` label (4.84:1 at its lightest end, 5.96:1 at the violet end). Secondary is `surface` with a `line` border; a secondary that leaves for GitHub is an `<a href>` with a 12px arrow in muted.
- Hover lifts 1px; primary takes `card-hover`, secondary takes `card`; pressed drops back to rest. Focus is a 2px `focus` ring, warm near-ink, at 2px offset on every button and link, the gradient Confirm included (12.10:1 on surface-tint); the offset gap keeps it clear of the gradient.
- Confirm line: the `meta` style in muted. In sample mode it always leads with **sample** in ink 500: "sample · would comment on #84120, #84077, #84201". That one word, in the same place in every state, is the whole disclosure: the flow runs end to end on real GitHub reads, and nothing is ever written.
- Result row: once the buttons give way, the action area takes the list row rhythm: a 10px mark on a 14px 500 title, the confirm line 2px below it, a link or Try again on the right. Links are 13px 500 in `accent-text` (4.99:1 on surface-tint, where `accent` measures 4.49:1), underlined at 35% and fully on hover. A pseudo-element pads the hit area to 36px, so the row never moves and the focus ring still hugs the text.
- Density: side padding, card top and bottom follow the system table. Copy to actions is `space-3`, `space-4`, `space-5`; actions to the confirm line and question to buttons are `space-2`, `space-3`, `space-3`. Button height and label size are the only sizes that change.
- Idle: heading, copy, primary and secondary, the confirm line saying what would happen.
- Confirming: the primary never acts alone. A question in 14px 500 ink appears between copy and buttons, identifiers in mono at .85em; the buttons become Confirm and Cancel; the confirm line says "sample · drafts only, nothing is posted to GitHub". It lives in the card, never in a dialog.
- Working: the warming arc and a muted title that counts ("Drafting comment 2 of 3"); the confirm line names the current target; the card's 3px bottom edge fills as each one lands, and the bottom padding gains those 3px.
- Done: the good mark and the result in ink ("Drafted 3 comments"), the confirm line saying what happened ("sample · nothing was posted to GitHub") and a link to what was made: View drafts opens the drawer with each draft. In live mode the same row reads "Posted 3 comments · on #84120, #84077, #84201 · just now · View comments"; a single target links straight to it on GitHub.
- Error: the unavailable mark, what failed in ink ("Couldn't draft the comments"), the cause in the confirm line ("GitHub returned 502 on #84077"), Try again on the right. Never `crit`. A live partial failure says what did post: "Posted 1 of 3 comments · #84077 and #84201 failed", and Try again retries only those.
- Skeleton: while the model is still writing, `line-soft` blocks for the heading, two copy lines, two buttons at their real height and radius, and the confirm line, so nothing shifts when the words land. The tint stays (it is ground, not accent); the gradient arrives with the words. `aria-busy` plus a hidden "Preparing a suggested action."
- Empty and warming do not apply. With nothing worth doing the composer leaves the card out rather than render an empty proposal. The card reads nothing from GitHub's stats endpoints, so it never waits on a 202; working borrows the warming mark and bar because the reader waits on GitHub the same way.
- Enter: the last piece on the screen. The card rises 10px over 550ms, then blooms (700ms up, 700ms down); heading, copy and confirm line wipe; the buttons arrive with the card and never animate on their own. Nothing bounces. Exit is the reverse: bloom collapses, card sinks.
- Update, always caused by a click or a response: to confirming, the question wipes in and the card grows with a layout transition that carries the buttons down while their labels swap; Cancel reverses it. To working, the question wipes out, the result row's title wipes in and the bar extends per target while the count runs. To done, the bar completes and retracts, the title rewipes and the card blooms once. Error has no bloom; Try again returns to working.
- Morph: a rewrite that keeps the same card id and the same `effect` keeps its state. If the effect changed, the old card exits and a new one enters, so a pending confirmation never silently changes its targets. A card that is working or done survives a rewrite untouched: that action is the reader's now, not the model's.
- Accessibility: a `section` labelled by its heading. Buttons are real `button` elements and leaving for GitHub is a real `a href`. Buttons are 36, 40 and 44px tall: 36px is the floor at any density (well past WCAG 2.5.8's 24px for a pointer-first desktop screen), and 44px at spacious, the density the composer picks for touch and presenting. Links pad their hit area to 36px. Entering confirming moves focus to Confirm, which reads the question through `aria-describedby`; Escape or Cancel returns focus to the primary. Confirm ignores activation for its first 400ms, and the question pushes the buttons down by a line, so a double click lands on the question, not on Confirm. The result row is a polite `role="status"`; focus moves to it while working, then to View drafts or Try again. Reduced motion collapses every move to a 200ms opacity change and skips the bloom.
