# Distribution

A histogram of how long something takes, with one ink marker that answers "how long would I wait" in words and a quieter p90 rule for the tail.

- The composer picks Distribution for wait questions asked from the reader's side: "how long until a maintainer reviews my PR", "how long do merges take here". It is not for change over time (LineChart) or two periods side by side (Comparison).
- Schema the model reads: `title` (sentence case, the model's words), `meta` (the window, such as "last 90 days"), `measure` (`firstReview`, `merge`, `issueResponse`), `population` (`firstTime`, `all`), `perspective` (`reader` by default, or `neutral`), `showP90` (true by default), `emptyTone` (`neutral` by default, or `good`) and an `empty` line. The model never sends bin edges, counts, positions or label wording.
- The system decides everything measured: one fixed bin ladder (under 1h, 1 to 4h, 4 to 12h, 12 to 24h, 1 to 3d, 3 to 7d, over 7d) so the same question always draws the same axis; counts from the data tool; the median and p90 from raw durations, never from the bins; the y scale (a round ceiling and two gridlines); and every label.
- Marker wording comes from `perspective` and width: "You would wait here · about 19h" at wide, "Your wait · about 19h" at narrow, "Median · about 19h" when neutral. Values round to minutes under an hour, hours under two days, days after that, so the label always says "about".
- Card: `g-card` with a `g-head` (title in `card-title`, window in `meta`). The body takes `card-y` top and bottom and `inset` on the sides.
- Lane: a 20px row above the plot, `space-2` to the plot top, that holds only the marker label, so the label never meets a bar.
- Median rule: 2px `ink`, drawn behind the bars, so it reads as a stem rising out of the bin that holds the median, up to the cap in the lane. It sits at that bin's center: on an ordinal axis a position inside the bin would be an interpolation the chart cannot back up, and a rule crossing a bar off center leaves 2px slivers. The label carries the exact value.
- Cap: an 8px `ink` dot with a 2px `surface` ring. The label is 13px, weight 500, `ink`, the number in 700, and sits right of the cap as a flag, or left of it when it would cross the content edge.
- p90 rule: 1px `--data-ref`, behind the bars, from the plot top, no cap. Its label "90% by 6d" is the `axis` style in `muted`, `space-2` beside the rule, and flips like the marker label. It is quieter than the median on every channel: width, color, height and type. It shows at wide only; at narrow the source line carries it.
- Bars: one series in `--data-1`, 24px wide, a 4px rounded data end and a square end on the baseline, centered in seven equal bins. They are never colored by value or by side of the marker, because the slow tail is the part a newcomer most needs to see.
- Grid: `line-soft` 1px at two round counts, with count labels in the `axis` style in a `space-5` gutter on the content edge. Wide only.
- Axis: a `line` baseline, 4px `line` ticks at the six bin edges, and edge labels (1h, 4h, 12h, 24h, 3d, 7d) in the `axis` style, `space-1` under the tick. Edge labels fit every width and need no dash, which range labels would.
- Source: `meta` in `muted`, saying how many items and who is counted, `stack` below the axis.
- Density: the plot height is `--plot-h`, 160px compact, 200px comfortable and 240px spacious. It is shared with LineChart, so the two plots line up when the composer puts them side by side. Padding follows the density table. Bars stay 24px at every density; the bins share whatever width the card has.
- Width: wide is 480px of card and up. Narrow, a one-third column, drops the counts, gridlines and p90 rule, keeps the edge labels, shortens the marker label and moves "90% reviewed by 6d" into the source line. If a bin falls under 32px, every other edge label drops.
- Hover: the whole bin is the target, not the 24px bar. It takes the system hover: the bar lifts onto a `surface-tint` shape `space-2` either side of it, `radius-md` at the top and square on the baseline, with no shadow. The gridlines it crosses hide, as row hairlines do, and its two edge labels turn `ink`. The tooltip sits `space-3` above the bar: `surface`, `radius-md`, the `card` shadow, the count in 600 with a 12px `--data-1` line key, then the bin and share in `meta`. It clamps to the content edge and never enters the lane.
- Focus: each bin is a `<button>` with its count and range in words. The chart is one tab stop and the arrow keys move between bins. The 2px `focus` ring hugs the bar at 2px offset, so the tooltip can sit above it clear of the ring. Focus shows the same tooltip as hover.
- Every state keeps the chart's full height at its density, so a Distribution beside a LineChart stays level while it loads, fails or warms, and nothing jumps when data lands.
- Skeleton: `line-soft` blocks at the real geometry (seven 24px bars with the 4px data end, six edge labels, the source line). The gutter stays reserved. No baseline, grid or marker until data lands. `aria-busy` plus a hidden sentence.
- Empty: one row in the list rhythm, centered in the plot's height, with a neutral or good mark by `emptyTone` and a real sentence ("No first-time contributor PRs reviewed yet" with "2 still open · oldest waiting 5d"). No axis, because a baseline with nothing on it reads as seven zeros. A zero is data: a window where every PR was reviewed within an hour draws one bar and six empty bins.
- Error: the unavailable mark, what failed in `ink`, the cause in `meta` and Try again in `accent-text` on the right, with the `focus` ring on keyboard focus. Never `crit`.
- Warming (the GitHub 202 wait): the warming mark, what GitHub is counting in `muted`, when it checks again in `meta`, and the card's bottom edge fills with the accent gradient as progress, the same 3px as a Metric bar.
- Enter: the card rises; the baseline and gridlines extend from the left; bars grow from the baseline left to right, 40ms apart (extend); the median stem draws up out of its bar as that bar lands, the cap settles with `power3.out` and the label wipes in; the p90 rule draws and its label wipes 160ms later. Exit is the strict reverse: labels wipe out, the stem erases back into its bar, bars retract right to left, the card sinks. Nothing on a Distribution bounces: the system bounces only the single latest or peak point a component marks, and the median is neither.
- Update: bars extend or retract to their new heights and the count labels count. If the median moves to another bin, its stem erases into the old bar and draws out of the new one while the number in the label counts.
- Morph (survives a rewrite, such as wide to narrow or a perspective change): bins re-space with Flip; dropped parts retract (gridlines) or erase (p90); the marker label and source line wipe to their new wording. Nothing remounts.
- Accessibility: the figure is a group whose `aria-label` states the finding ("Half of first-time contributors got a first review within 19 hours. 1 in 10 waited more than 6 days."). A table view is available: a visually hidden table of every bin and count rides inside the figure, and the screen's table toggle shows it. Tooltips enhance and never gate. Contrast: bars 4.84:1 on `surface` and 4.49:1 on the hover shape, `ink` rule 16.2:1, `data-ref` rule 3.30:1 on `surface` and 3.06:1 on the hover shape, axis and source text 5.90:1, `focus` ring 13.04:1, Try again in `accent-text` 5.38:1. Reduced motion collapses every move to a 200ms opacity change.
