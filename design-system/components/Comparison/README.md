# Comparison

Two periods side by side for two to four measures, each a pair of bars on its own zero-based scale with a signed delta and a verdict in a fixed column on the right.

- The composer picks Comparison for "is it better or worse than last month" questions: the same measures read across two equal windows. One measure with a delta is a Metric; a trend over many periods is a LineChart.
- Schema: `title`, `window` (`week`, `month`, `30d`, `milestone`), `pairs` (2 to 4), `finding` (one sentence for the aria-label, 90 characters at most), `emptyTone` (`neutral` or `good`). Each pair: `measure` (an id from the measure catalog, such as `review-wait-median`, `prs-merged`, `ci-p50`, `failing-check-rate`), `direction` (`lower-is-better`, `higher-is-better`, `neutral`), `notable` (boolean), optional `ask` (a follow-up question).
- The model chooses meaning only: which measures, which window, the direction and whether the change is notable. The system fetches both values, computes the delta, writes the period labels, the sign, the verdict word and its tone. No number, color or word crosses from the model.
- Tone: a notable change that helps reads `better` in `good`; a notable change that hurts reads `worse` in `crit`; a change the model marks not notable reads `steady` in `muted`; an exact zero reads `0%` and `no change` in `muted`. A `neutral` measure reads `higher` or `lower`, always `muted`. Up is good or bad by the measure, never by the sign.
- Header: `g-head`, card-title on the left, the legend on the right in `meta`, `muted`: current period first, then previous, each keyed by a 12 by 8px bar with the bar's rounded end. `space-2` from key to label, `space-3` between items.
- Row: three columns, name 156px, bars fluid, delta 96px, `space-4` gutters. Two 18px lines `space-1` apart: line 1 is now and the answer (name, current bar, delta), line 2 is before and the context (meta, previous bar, verdict). Row height 56, 64 or 72px.
- Name: `row-title` in `ink` on line 1, a `meta` line in `muted` on line 2, one line each with ellipsis. Names are capped at 24 characters.
- Bars: 8px, square baseline end, 4px rounded data end. Current in `data-1` on line 1, previous in `data-ref` on line 2. Zero based with one scale per row, so the length ratio is the change; the larger value spans the column less 64px kept for its tip value. A 1px `line` baseline at zero runs the height of the pair and lines up down the card.
- Tip values: 13px sans, tabular, `space-2` after the bar end. Current at 600 in `ink`, previous at 500 in `muted`. Units in mono 11px `muted`, `space-1` after the number. Rates print as percent.
- Delta: 15px, 700, tabular, always signed with + or U+2212. Percent for durations and counts (whole numbers, one decimal under 1%), points for rates. Its right edge sits on the content edge, so every delta aligns down the card. The verdict sits under it in `meta` at 500 in the same tone.
- Hairlines: `line` between rows, inset to the side padding; `line-soft` under the header and over the footer.
- Footer: `meta` in `muted`. The source on the left is always present ("from 214 merged PRs · 3,480 check runs"); the tally of verdicts on the right closes the delta column.
- Density: side padding, row padding and card padding follow the density table; the three columns, the bars, the type and the delta stay fixed, so the delta column lands on the same edge as every card above or below it.
- Skeleton: `line-soft` blocks at the real line and bar heights, one row per requested pair, header and footer blocks, no hairlines, no baseline and no accent until data lands. `aria-busy` with a visually hidden "Loading the comparison."
- Empty: header with the window in meta, then one row in the list rhythm with a neutral or good mark by `emptyTone`. Empty means there is no value to compare (a median of zero reviews), never a zero: a count of 0 still draws as data.
- Error: header, then one row with the unavailable mark, what failed in `ink`, the cause in `meta`, Try again in `accent-text` on the delta edge. Never `crit`: a failed request is not data.
- Warming (the GitHub 202 wait): header, then one row with the warming mark, what GitHub is counting in `muted`, when it checks again in `meta`, and the card's 3px bottom edge filling with the accent gradient as progress.
- Hover: the system's shared row hover. The whole row is the target and lifts onto `surface-tint` as an inset rounded shape (`radius-md`, `space-2` in from the card edge, `space-1` in from the hairlines), and the hairlines above and below it hide. No shadow. `data-ref` holds 3.06:1 on the tint.
- Tooltip: opens `space-2` to the right of the hovered row's current bar tip, centered on the row and inside it, so it never covers another row and never leaves the card, last row included. `surface`, `radius-md`, `card` shadow, `line-soft` border. Two lines that mirror the row: a 12 by 3px line key, the value at 600 and the short period in `muted`, then the absolute change over the verdict in its tone. It sits over the row's own tip values and verdict and restates them.
- Focus: when a pair carries an `ask`, its row is a button; keyboard focus draws the 2px `focus` ring, 2px offset, on the same rounded shape and opens the same tooltip.
- Enter: card rises; rows slide in from the left 70ms apart and their hairlines extend; in each row the previous bar extends first and the current bar 160ms after it, so the change plays as a story; values count with their bars; the delta counts from zero as the current bar lands and the verdict wipes in. Exit is the reverse: verdict wipes out, bars retract current first, rows slide out, card sinks.
- Update: bars extend or retract to the new lengths, values and delta count, the verdict wipes to its new word and its tone crossfades. A rescaled row moves both bars at once.
- Morph: when the window steps forward a period, the current bar recolors to `data-ref` and moves to line 2 with Flip, the old previous bar retracts, and the new current bar extends on line 1. Pairs that join or leave slide in or out while their neighbors move with Flip. Nothing bounces: Comparison has no latest or peak point.
- Accessibility: the card is a figure whose `aria-label` states the finding ("Review wait rose 38% in September while failing checks fell 2.2 points"), not the chart type. Bars are hidden from assistive tech; each row reads its values as text ("19.6 hours in September, 14.2 in August") followed by the delta and verdict. A table view of measure, previous, current and change is available. Direction never rests on color: the sign carries direction and the verdict word carries meaning. Reduced motion shows the final state with a 200ms fade.
