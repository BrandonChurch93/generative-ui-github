# Heatmap

> Build note (from `docs/schemas.md`, which wins on who supplies what): key swatch radius is `radius-xs`, not 3px; cells and keys use tokens only.

A punch card of when work happens: seven weekday rows by 24 hour columns in the viewer's time zone, each cell's count drawn as a step on one coral heat ramp.

- The composer picks Heatmap for "when does this team work" and "when do reviews happen" questions, where the answer is a weekly rhythm rather than a trend. Change over time goes to LineChart; who carries the work goes to ContributorRank.
- Schema the model reads: `title` (sentence case, e.g. "When reviews happen"), `unit` (enum: `commits`, `reviews`, `merges`, `comments`), `window` (enum: `4w`, `12w`, `52w`), `summary` (the finding in one sentence, used as the chart's `aria-label`), `emptyTone` (`neutral` or `good`, default `neutral`) and an `empty` line. The counts never come from the model: the data layer fills a 7 × 24 matrix from GitHub (`stats/punch_card` for commits, review events for reviews), shifted into the viewer's zone and reordered Monday first.
- The system decides everything visual: each count's step, the peak, the zone label, the hour cadence, the cell size and the tooltip wording ("27 reviews", "1 commit").
- Frame: `g-card`, the header in `card-title`, the window and zone abbreviation in `meta` on the right ("last 12 weeks · MDT").
- Days: Mon to Sun top to bottom, `axis` style in `muted`, centered on each row. The peak cell's day is in `ink` at weight 500.
- Cells: 24 columns share the plot width at 1fr and stay square, with 2px `surface` gaps. Radius 3px at compact and 4px otherwise, about a fifth of the cell. At the sheet's card widths (540, 600, 668) that is 18, 20 and 22px.
- Ramp: `heat-0` (#f3efec) is zero; counts take `heat-1` to `heat-5` (#ffcec2, #f5ac9c, #e88671, #d4614a, #b44029) by step = ceil(5 × count ÷ peak). The peak is always `heat-5`. With a peak of 4 or less a single event lands on `heat-2` or darker, so sparse data never sits one faint step from empty.
- Hours: `axis` style in `muted`, 24-hour clock in the viewer's zone, centered under their cell, `space-2` below the grid. Every 3 hours; every 6 at compact.
- Peak: a 4px `accent-ink` dot centered in the peak cell (5.67:1 on `heat-5`), keyed in the footer by the same glyph and "Peak · Tue 14:00 · 38 reviews" in `meta`. Ties put the dot on every tied cell and the footer names the first ("Peak · Tue 14:00 and 2 more · 38 reviews").
- Legend: footer left, in `meta`. The zero swatch stands apart from the five steps, then the low count, five 12px swatches (radius 3px, 2px gaps) and the high count, which is the peak.
- Hover: the cell lifts 1.2× on a 2px `surface` ring. The tooltip sits `space-2` above it: `surface`, `radius-md`, `card` shadow, `line-soft` border, the count in 600 with the unit in `muted`, day and hour in `meta` below, and a 10px key in the cell's step. On the top two rows it flips below the cell so the card never clips it.
- Density: compact puts `space-2` between days and grid, labels hours every 6 and leaves `space-3` above the footer; comfortable `space-3`, every 3, `space-4`; spacious `space-4`, every 3, `space-5`. Side and top padding follow the shared density table, so the header title, day labels and legend share one content edge, and the grid's right edge meets the header meta.
- Skeleton: `line-soft` blocks at the real geometry: header blocks, day and hour label blocks, seven row bars the height of the grid rows, two footer blocks. Bars rather than 168 cells, so loading never reads as a week of zeros. The skeleton card is the same height as the loaded one.
- Empty: the header renders, then one row in the StatusList rhythm with the `emptyTone` mark, a real sentence ("No reviews in the last 12 weeks") and a meta line. No grid. A cell with zero is data; empty is only a window with nothing to place.
- Error: header, then the unavailable mark, what failed in ink, the cause in meta and Try again in `accent-text` on the right (`g-retry`). Never `crit`.
- Warming (GitHub 202 on `stats/punch_card`): header, then the warming mark, "GitHub is counting commits by hour" in `muted`, when it will check again in meta, and the card's bottom edge fills with the accent gradient as progress.
- Enter: the card rises (550ms). The heat layer then wipes across columns from 00 to 23 (wipe, 900ms `power3.inOut`), clipped from the plot's left edge, so hours arrive in the order they happen; zero cells ride with it. Arriving from the skeleton, the wipe replaces the row bars left to right. As the wipe passes the peak column the peak dot pops with `back.out(2)`; it is the one mark in this component that bounces, because nothing bounces except the point a component marks as the latest or the peak. The legend swatches extend left to right (700ms) and the peak line wipes in after them. Day and hour labels arrive with the card as frame.
- Exit: the strict reverse. The peak line wipes out, the legend retracts, the dot shrinks, the heat layer wipes out left to right (500ms `power2.in`), the card sinks.
- Update: a changed cell tweens to its new step over 800ms `power2.out`, the color following the count; the legend's high count counts; if the peak moves, the dot shrinks out of the old cell and pops into the new one. Nothing wipes.
- Morph (survives a rewrite, e.g. reviews to commits, or 12 weeks to 4): re-emphasize, never redraw. Cells tween to their new steps column by column, 20ms apart from 00; the title and window wipe out and in; the legend's high count counts. A density change moves cells and labels with Flip.
- Reduced motion: every move becomes a 200ms opacity change; the dot and legend appear with the grid.
- Accessibility: the plot's `aria-label` is the model's `summary` ("Reviews peak Tuesday at 14:00 and thin out after 19:00 and on weekends."). The grid is `role="grid"` with seven rows; one tab stop enters at the peak cell, arrow keys move by hour and day, Home and End jump to 00 and 23, and each cell is named "Tuesday 14:00, 38 reviews". The tooltip follows focus. Focus draws a 2px `focus` ring at 2px offset on a 5px `surface` halo (13.04:1 there), so the ring never lands on a dark neighbor or reads as coral data.
- Table view: the light steps sit under 3:1 by design (`heat-1` 1.41:1, `heat-2` 1.86:1, `heat-3` 2.60:1 on surface), so the reader's relief is the tooltip, the legend with its zero swatch, and a table view that is always available: the same 7 × 24 counts as a `<table>` with day row headers, hour column headers and a total per day, counts in mono.
