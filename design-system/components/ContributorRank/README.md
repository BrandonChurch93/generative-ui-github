# ContributorRank

Who carries a repo's work: one ranked share bar per person, with the smallest group that carries half the work, the bus factor, set apart as the load-bearing wall.

- The composer picks it for "who's carrying this repo" questions: who merges, who reviews, how concentrated the work is. Not for activity over time (LineChart) or a single headcount (Metric).
- Schema: `title`, optional `meta`, `measure` (`commits` or `reviews`, the unit the counts and the band speak in), `people` as the tool returns them (handle, optional display name, count), `limit` (3 to 8 rows), `wallTone` (`fact` or `risk`, default `fact`), `emptyTone` and an `empty` line. The window is the last 90 days, set by the data layer. The model chooses `title`, `limit`, `wallTone` and `emptyTone`. It never writes a share, the bus factor or the band's sentence: the system sorts by count, computes each share of the window's whole total, finds the wall, scales the bars and writes the band from a fixed template.
- Wall: the smallest group from the top whose shares together reach 50% of the window's total, counting everyone, not just the rows shown. A tie at the boundary goes to whoever was active most recently.
- Row: the handle in JetBrains Mono, 13px, weight 500, ink, never truncated before the name is; the optional display name 13px, weight 400, `muted`, `space-2` after the handle, truncating first; the count in `meta`, `muted`, in a 40px right-aligned column; the share in 13.5px, weight 600, tabular, ink, whole percents, in a 36px column on the content edge. The rank bar sits under that line. The text line is an 18px box, so rows land on whole pixels: 46, 58 and 66px.
- Rank bar: `space-2` tall, square baseline end on the content edge, `radius-sm` data end. Its length is the person's count over the top count, so the top share fills the content width and the printed percent carries the absolute value. Never shorter than `space-2`. Behind every bar runs a track in `line-soft`, the bar's shape at full content width: the leader's length, so every other row reads as a part of it. Wall bars are `data-1` (accent, 4.84:1 on surface); everyone else is `data-ref` (3.30:1). Text never wears a bar color.
- Band: one row slot directly under the wall, where the row hairline would be. A key shaped like a bar (`space-3` by `space-2`, `data-1`), "Bus factor N" in ink at weight 500, then the finding in `muted` from a fixed template ("these two carry 52% of merged commits", "one person carries 61% of reviews"), then a `line` hairline running on to the content edge. In a narrow frame the sentence wraps and the key and rule stay on its first line.
- Risk: `wallTone: risk` is the model saying the concentration is the problem it is answering. The key becomes a dashed `warn-fill` mark, a `warn` tag "at risk" ends the band and the card's label leads with "At risk". The bars stay `data-1` because the wall is still the subject. No hot card and never `crit`: a concentrated repo is not a blocked one.
- Spread out: when the rows shown never reach half, nothing is accented. The band moves under the last row with a `data-ref` key and reads "Bus factor above 5 · the top five carry 41% of merged commits".
- Foot: `meta` in `muted` under a `line` hairline, only when people are left out ("41 more authors share the other 9% · 78 commits"), so the shares on the card always close to 100%.
- Padding and hairlines follow StatusList: the density's side padding for header, rows, band and foot, so every edge lines up with the cards above and below; `line`, 1px, inset to the side padding, between rows inside a group; the header rule is `line-soft`, full width.
- Density: side padding `space-4`, `space-5`, `space-6`; row top and bottom `space-2`, `space-3`, `space-4`; name to bar `space-1` at compact and `space-2` above it. Nothing else changes, so a density switch never re-ranks or rescales.
- Hover: a row with a follow-up question ("What is huozhi working on?") is a button. It takes the system row hover: it lifts onto `surface-tint`, an inset rounded shape (`radius-md`, inset `space-2` from the card edge), and the hairlines above and below it hide. No shadow. Bars keep their color: `data-ref` holds 3.06:1 and `data-1` 4.49:1 on the tint. The tooltip sits `space-3` after the bar's end, centered on it, and flips before the end when the bar passes 60% of the width: surface, `radius-md`, `card` shadow, a short line key in the series color, the count in 600 with its unit in `muted`, and the last activity in `meta`.
- Focus: the same row button takes the 2px `focus` ring with a 2px offset, on the hover ground's shape, and the hairlines above and below it hide as on hover.
- Skeleton: `line-soft` blocks at the real line heights (handle and name 9px, count 7px, share 9px) and bar blocks at the real `space-2` height, descending, so the ranking reads before the names land. No tracks, no hairlines, no band, no accent until data arrives. `aria-busy` plus a hidden "Loading commit authors."
- Empty: the header renders, then one row in the shared message rhythm: the `emptyTone` mark (neutral for this component in practice), a sentence naming the window ("Nothing merged on v3 in the last 90 days") and where the last work was in `meta`. No bars, no band.
- Error: the header renders, then the unavailable mark, what failed in ink, the cause in `meta` and Try again in `accent-text` where the share would sit. Never `crit`.
- Warming: GitHub answers 202 while it computes contributor stats. The header renders, then the warming mark, "GitHub is counting contributors" in `muted`, when it will check again in `meta`, and the card's bottom edge fills with the 3px accent gradient as progress.
- A person with no work in the window is not listed. An empty window is the empty state, never a list of zeros.
- Enter: the card rises; rows slide in from the left 70ms apart, top to bottom, each track extending with its row; each bar then extends over its track from its square end (700ms, `power3.inOut`) as its share counts up. Nothing here bounces: no single point is marked as the latest or the peak. The band takes its slot in the same stagger: its key extends, its sentence wipes and its rule extends to the content edge, so the wall is named just as its bars finish adding up past half. The foot wipes last.
- Exit: the strict reverse, bottom to top. The foot wipes out, bars retract to their square ends, tracks retract with their rows, the band's rule retracts and its sentence wipes out, rows slide out left, the card sinks.
- Update: bars extend or retract to their new lengths while counts and shares count; rows that change rank move with Flip. When the wall grows or shrinks, the band moves with Flip to its new slot, a bar crossing the boundary crossfades between `data-1` and `data-ref` over 300ms and the band's sentence wipes to its new wording.
- Morph: surviving a rewrite (commits to reviews, say), the title wipes, surviving people keep their rows and their bars move from the current length to the new one, never from zero; dropped people slide out and new ones slide in; the band moves with Flip. A switch of `wallTone` crossfades the key and the warn mark and slides the tag in.
- Accessibility: the card's `aria-label` states the finding ("Two people, ijjk and huozhi, carry 52% of merged commits in the last 90 days"), not the chart type. The wall and everyone else are two ordered lists, the second continuing the rank with `start`, and the band is a paragraph between them, so a screen reader hears the bus factor in place. Each row reads "ijjk, JJ Kasper, 268 merged commits, 31%"; bars are `aria-hidden`. A table view (person, count, share, running total) is available from the card. The wall is never color alone: it is also the position above the band and the band's words. `data-1` against `data-ref` holds ΔE 10.9 under deuteranopia and 16.8 for normal vision.
