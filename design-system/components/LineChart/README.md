# LineChart

> Build note (from `docs/schemas.md`, which wins on who supplies what): each series is `{ measure, name }` and `failures` is a boolean; the data layer supplies the weekly points and the failure counts. Key swatch radius is `radius-xs`.

A trend over weeks in a card: one or two lines on one y axis with the latest value at each line's end and, when failures matter, a strip of failed runs under the plot that shares its weeks.

- The composer picks LineChart when the shape over time is the answer: "is CI getting slower", "how has review wait changed", "did failures climb with it". A single current value is a Metric; a trend beside that value is a Sparkline on the Metric; two periods side by side are a Comparison; a wait distribution is a Distribution.
- Schema the model reads: `title`, `meta` (the statistic and the window, such as "p50 · last 12 weeks"), `unit` (`min`, `h`, `d`, `count`, `percent`), `series` (one or two, each a `name` and its weekly points; the first is the series the story is about), optional `failures` (failed runs per week, same weeks), `finding` (one sentence that states what the chart shows, 120 characters at most), `emptyTone` (`neutral` by default, or `good`) and an `empty` line. The model picks the series, their order and the sentence. It never sends colors, scales, ticks, label positions or widths.
- The system decides: the color by order (first `--data-1` coral, second `--data-2` slate, failures `--crit-fill`), a zero-based y scale on four equal intervals with round ticks, which weeks get labels, which strip columns get counts, the gutter, the end zone, every height and every position.
- Card: `g-card` with a `g-head` (title in `card-title`, meta in mono `muted`). The body takes `card-y` top and bottom and the density's side padding.
- Legend: only with two series or a failure strip (one series needs none; the title names it). It sits above the plot, `card-y` below it. Line key 16 × 2px in the series color with round caps; column key 8 × 12px in `crit-fill` with a 2px rounded top, mirroring the marks. Names in the `label` style in `muted`, never the series color. `space-4` between items, `space-2` from key to name. It wraps to a second line before it ever moves.
- Plot: 160, 200 or 240px by density. Gridlines `line-soft`, 1px, solid; the zero baseline in `line`. The plot is set down by half an axis label (6px) so the top tick never clips.
- Y ticks: the `axis` style in `muted`, right-aligned in a gutter as wide as the widest tick plus `space-2`, so the widest tick starts on the content edge under the title. Bare numbers; the unit rides the end label and the tooltip.
- Weeks: each week owns an equal band and its point sits at the band's center, so columns and centered labels never spill past the plot's left edge.
- Line: 2px, round joins and caps. `data-1` draws on top of `data-2`. Never more than two lines, never a third color, never `accent-2`.
- Endpoint: an 8px dot in the series color with a 2px `surface` ring. It marks the latest week, so it is the one thing on the card that bounces (`back.out(2)`).
- End label: the value in sans 13px 600 `ink`, tabular, then the unit in the `axis` style, `muted`, `space-1` after. It sits `space-3` after the dot, vertically centered on it, in an end zone as wide as the widest end label plus `space-3` (64px in the samples). When two end labels would touch, the lower one drops and the legend and tooltip carry it; labels are never nudged off their lines.
- X axis: the `axis` style in `muted`, centered under its week, `space-2` below the baseline. Labels thin by counting back from the latest week, so the endpoint's week is always labeled: every k weeks, the smallest k of 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 that leaves 64px between labels.
- Failure strip: its own band under the plot, `card-y` below the baseline, with its own `line` baseline and the shared week axis under it. Columns in `crit-fill`, 40% of the week's band and never wider than 24px, a 4px rounded top and a square base, 32, 40 or 48px tall at the week with the most failures. The count sits in the `axis` style 4px above each cap. There is no scale, so there is never a second y axis. Counts show on every column while a week's band is 32px or wider, otherwise on the first and latest week only.
- Widths: full (1280px, the shell's content width at 1440) labels every week and counts every column with 24px columns; half (632px) labels every second week, 16px columns; third (416px) labels every third week, counts the first and latest week, 10px columns. Plot height, ticks, gutter, end zone, end labels and the legend's place hold at every width.
- Density changes side padding, card top and bottom, the legend and strip gaps (`card-y`), plot height and strip height. Type sizes never change.
- Hover: a 1px crosshair in `line` snaps to the nearest week; every series gets an 8px hover dot with the `surface` ring; the hovered week's axis label turns `ink` at weight 500. One tooltip lists every series at that week: `surface`, `line-soft` border, `radius-md`, the `card` shadow, each row a 12 × 2px line key, the value in 13px 600 and the name in `muted`. It follows the pointer's height, sits `space-3` beside the crosshair on the side with room, and never covers the end labels.
- Focus: the chart is one tab stop with a 2px `focus` ring at 2px offset around the chart region. Arrow keys step the crosshair a week at a time, Home and End jump to the first and latest week, and the tooltip follows. Focus shows exactly what hover shows.
- Every state keeps the chart's full height at its density (the plot plus the week axis), so the card is one size whether it is loading, empty, failed, warming or drawn, and it sits level beside a Distribution.
- Skeleton: title and meta blocks, tick and week blocks at their real positions, gridlines and baseline in `line-soft`. No line, no endpoint, no accent until data lands. With two series the legend renders as two blocks. `aria-busy` plus a visually hidden sentence.
- Empty: one row in the list rhythm centered in the chart's height, starting on the content edge: a neutral or good mark by `emptyTone`, a real sentence in `row-title`, the reason in `meta` ("No full week of runs yet", "workflow added Oct 5 · first point lands Oct 12"). No axis and no ticks: nothing set a scale and a bare baseline reads as zeros. A zero is data: weeks with 0 failed runs draw a flat line on the baseline.
- Error: the same row with the unavailable mark, what failed in `ink`, the cause in `meta` and Try again in `accent-text` on the content edge. Never `crit`.
- Warming (the GitHub 202 wait): the warming mark, what GitHub is counting in `muted`, when it checks again in `meta`, and the card's bottom edge fills with the accent gradient as progress, the same 3px as a Metric bar.
- Enter: the card rises; the baseline extends; gridlines and ticks arrive with the card; failure columns grow from the strip baseline left to right, 40ms apart (extend, 700ms); the line draws (draw, 1100ms, `power2.inOut`), the second series 120ms behind the first; each endpoint pops with `back.out(2)` as its draw lands and its end value counts (800ms).
- Exit is the strict reverse: end values and dots shrink away, lines erase back to their first week (500ms), columns retract (350ms), the card sinks.
- Update (a new week closes): the weeks shift one band left with Flip, the oldest week's label slides out, the new segment draws from the old endpoint, the dot travels to the new end without a second pop and the end value counts. If the scale changes, gridlines hold still while tick labels count to their new values and the line morphs.
- Morph (survives a rewrite): no redraw. Paths morph in place (800ms, `power2.out`), ticks count, a series that leaves erases and one that arrives draws. Color follows the series, never its position, so a survivor never repaints.
- Accessibility: the chart region is `role="img"` with an `aria-label` that states the finding ("CI got 39% slower in 12 weeks: the median run went from 13.2 to 18.4 minutes."), written by the model and checked against the data. A table view is available: a visually hidden table of every week and series follows the chart, and the screen's table toggle shows it. Tooltips enhance and never gate. Keyboard steps are announced in a polite live region ("Week of Sep 7: new contributors 28 hours, core team 7.9 hours"). Identity never rests on color: a legend names every series, end labels sit on their lines, and the failure strip is a separate band with a different mark shape (data-1 and crit-fill are only 3.6 apart in ΔE, so hue alone could never carry it).
- Contrast on `surface`: `data-1` 4.84:1, `data-2` 5.30:1, `crit-fill` 6.62:1 (1.37:1 against `data-1`, separated by a 20° hue step), axis and legend text in `muted` 5.90:1, end values in `ink` 16.2:1, Try again in `accent-text` 5.38:1, focus ring 13.04:1. Reduced motion collapses every move to a 200ms opacity change.
