# Steps

An ordered path the model writes from the repo's own data, drawn as numbered rings on a hairline spine, each step an action title with a line or two of body, an optional fact and an optional link.

- The composer picks Steps when the answer is a sequence the reader walks in order: "where would a new contributor start", "how do I run this locally", "how do I cut a release". When order does not matter it picks a list instead. With fewer than two steps it writes an Insight.
- Schema: `title` (the card header, written by the model), optional `meta` (a repo, branch or scope on the right) and `steps`, 2 to 6. Each step has a stable `id`, a `title` (imperative, 40 characters at most), a `body` (one or two sentences, 140 characters at most, backticks mark code), an optional `fact` (24 characters at most: a count, a duration or a command, copied from the tool results, never estimated) and an optional `link` with a `label` (28 characters at most) and either `href` (leaves the app) or `ask` (a follow-up question that recomposes the screen). The system decides the numbering, the accent, the glyph, the clamping and every size and space.
- Number: a 21px ring in 1px `line`, the numeral in mono 11px weight 500, `muted`. The ring sits 1px below the title's line box so it centers on the letters, not the box. The first step's ring is `accent` and its numeral `accent-text`: the model chose where to begin, so the start is the one thing in the card that wears the brand. Numbers are order, not state, so no state color touches them, and nothing marks a step as done or current.
- Spine: 1px `line` through the ring centers, `space-1` clear of each ring, none under the last step. It is a hairline that draws in, so `line`, not `line-soft`, and it replaces the row rules a list would have.
- Title: the `action` style, 15px weight 600, `ink`, one line with ellipsis. Its line height is 21px, the ring's diameter, so title and ring share a center.
- Fact: `meta` in `muted`, on the right edge and on the title's baseline: the same title left, meta right rhythm as the card header.
- Body: the `body` style, 14px, `muted`, `space-1` under the title, two lines at most, `text-wrap: pretty`. A muted body lets the titles alone read as the plan.
- Code: mono at 0.85em, weight 500, `ink`, no chip and no tint, the same rule as Insight. A command never breaks across lines and takes 0.1em on each side so the sans word space does not close up against it.
- Link: the shared `g-link`, 13px weight 500 in `accent-text`, underline at 35% `accent-text` with a 3px offset (the Try again treatment), `space-2` under the body, `space-3` at spacious. When it leaves the app a 12px up-right arrow follows: 1.5px stroke, round caps, `currentColor`, `space-1` after the text. An `ask` link stays in the app and has no arrow. Hover takes the underline to full `accent-text` and steps the arrow 1px up and right. Focus is a 2px `focus` ring, 2px offset, 4px corners, as on every `g-link`.
- Card: `g-card` with the standard header (`card-title` left, `meta` right, a full-width `line-soft` rule), the same as StatusList. Side padding is the density's shared value, so the ring's left edge sits under the header title and on the edge every StatusList row uses. Text starts 33px in: the ring plus `space-3`.
- Density: side padding `space-4`, `space-5`, `space-6`. Between steps, and above and below the list, `space-4`, `space-5`, `space-6`. Body to link `space-2`, `space-2`, `space-3`. Type sizes do not change.
- Skeleton: the header as two blocks, then three steps of `line-soft` blocks at real line heights (title 10px, body 8px, fact 7px, link 8px) with a filled `line-soft` disc where the ring goes. No accent ring and no spine until steps land.
- Error: the header renders, then the shared StatusList error row: unavailable mark, what failed in ink, the cause in meta, Try again in `accent-text` on the right. Never `crit`; crit is for data that blocks.
- Warming (the GitHub 202 wait): the header renders, then the shared row with the warming mark, a muted line naming what GitHub is counting and meta saying when it will check again. The card's bottom edge fills with the accent gradient as progress, the same 3px as a Metric bar.
- Empty does not apply. The model writes the steps, so nothing fetched comes back as an empty list; with fewer than two steps the composer writes an Insight. A zero is data: a step that would read 0 open is dropped, not drawn.
- Enter: the card rises, then steps slide in from the left 70ms apart, top to bottom. Each spine segment extends downward from its ring as the next step arrives, so the path draws itself to the last step. Exit is the reverse: segments retract upward, steps slide out left starting from the last, the card sinks.
- Update: a fact's number counts to its new value; changed words crossfade over 200ms.
- Morph: steps diff by `id`. Survivors move with Flip and their numerals count to their new position. Dropped steps slide out and their segment retracts; new steps slide in and their segment extends. When a different step becomes first, the accent crossfades onto its ring.
- Nothing in Steps bounces: it marks no latest or peak point.
- Reduced motion: every move becomes a 200ms opacity change and numbers jump to their final value.
- Accessibility: an `ol` with `role="list"`, since some browsers drop list semantics when the markers are removed. Each title is an `h3` under the card's `h2` and carries a visually hidden "Step 1 of 4." so heading navigation walks the path; the ring numeral is hidden from assistive tech. External links add "(opens GitHub)" for screen readers. Title 16.19:1, body, numeral and fact 5.90:1, the `accent-text` numeral and link 5.38:1, all on `surface`; the `accent` ring 4.84:1. The ring and spine are decoration; the numeral carries the order.
